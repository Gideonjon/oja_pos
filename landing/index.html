How it works

A sale in four taps.

No training and no extra hardware. If you can use a calculator, you can run the till.

  1. Type the amount

    The keypad is the first thing you see.

  2. Pick a method

  4. Payment received

    Shown only once the money has arrived.

Security

Built so the money adds up.

The rules that protect your balance live on the server, where the app cannot bend them.

No payment keys in the app

Paystack and bill-payment keys stay on the server. A copied app has nothing to steal.

The app cannot edit your float

Only the server credits or debits it, after checking with the payment provider.

Every payment counts once

A payment confirmed twice is still credited once, and two purchases at the same instant cannot overdraw you.

Failed bills refund themselves

A definite failure returns the money. An unclear answer is held as processing until it is settled.
